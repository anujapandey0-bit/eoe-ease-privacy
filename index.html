Contact: anuja.pandey0@gmail.com. SUMMARY • EoE Ease does not require an account. • The information you enter stays on your device. • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising. 1. INFORMATION YOU ENTER IN THE APP The App lets you record information such as: • Your eliminated food groups and diet phase (elimination, reintroduction, maintenance) • Meal descriptions, symptoms, severity ratings, and notes in the journal • Dates such as the start of a diet phase or your next endoscopy • Ingredient text you paste or capture, and the results of ingredient checks • Reintroduction log entries This may include information about your health. We treat it as sensitive. 2. WHERE YOUR INFORMATION IS STORED This information is stored locally on your device. We do not receive it, and we do not have servers that store it. [IF YOU ADD BACKUP OR SYNC: If you turn on backup or sync, your data is stored in your personal cloud account (for example, Apple iCloud) under that provider's terms.] 3. CAMERA AND PHOTOS If you use the camera or choose a photo to read an ingredient label, the App uses it to recognise the text. We do not store label photos unless you choose to save them. 4. INFORMATION WE COLLECT AUTOMATICALLY We do not collect analytics, usage data, device identifiers, or crash reports. Apple may collect limited information when you download the App or use TestFlight, under Apple's own privacy policy. 5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION Because your entries stay on your device, the App uses them only to provide the features you asked for: showing your journal and history, running ingredient checks, creating charts, and generating your summary. We may disclose information if required by law, but since we do not hold your entries, we would normally have nothing to disclose. 7.
